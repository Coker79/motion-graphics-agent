Did you know a loaf of bread in Germany once cost 200 billion marks?

In 1923, Weimar Germany printed money to cover its war reparations. Hyperinflation sent prices doubling every few days, workers were paid twice a day, and one US dollar became worth over 4 trillion marks. A currency collapse doesn't happen overnight. It happens when people stop believing in the money.

- Exchange rate November 1923: $1 = 4.2 trillion marks
- Peak monthly inflation in October 1923 meant prices doubled roughly every 3–4 days
