Did you know Japan's stock market took 34 years to recover from one crash?

In 1989, the land under Tokyo's Imperial Palace was said to be worth more than all of California. The Nikkei had tripled and everyone believed prices could only go up. It peaked at 38,915 on December 29, 1989, then crashed, and didn't get back there until February 2024. A stock market crash can last a lifetime. Remember Tokyo.
