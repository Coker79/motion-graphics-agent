Did you know owning gold was illegal for most Americans for over 40 years?

In 1933, Executive Order 6102 forced Americans to hand in their gold at $20.67 an ounce, with up to 10 years in prison for refusing. Months later, Washington raised gold to $35, and the US dollar lost about 40% of its gold value overnight. The government didn't take your money. It changed what your money was worth.

Sources:
- Executive Order 6102, April 5, 1933 (gold surrender at $20.67/oz; penalties up to $10,000 fine and/or 10 years)
- Gold Reserve Act of January 1934 (gold revalued to $35/oz)
- Private gold ownership restored in the US on December 31, 1974
